## Architectural Decisions & Notes

- Backend confirmed as Node.js + Express + TypeScript in Strict Mode.
- Endpoints confirmed as dedicated `/api/v1/*` routes.
- Binary operations strictly require `{ a, b }`.
- Unary operations (`sqrt`, `percentage`) require `{ a }` with `b` omitted/optional.
- Percentage defined strictly as unary `a / 100`.
- IEEE 754 precision mitigation strictly specified as bounding to 10 decimal places with trailing zero truncation.
